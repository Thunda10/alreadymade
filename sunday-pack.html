The Sunday Pack

Sunday's sermon, ready to share by Monday.

Every week we turn your message into short captioned clips, quote graphics, a written summary and a discussion guide for small groups. There's no software to learn and there are no meetings. If your sermons already go out as a podcast, there's nothing to upload either.

See the prices See a sample pack

What you get

Five things arrive every Monday.

  • 013 vertical clips with captions, 30 to 75 seconds each. The free sample is audio over your sermon artwork. Once you share a video link, the clips show your speaker.
  • 023 quote graphics.
  • 03A 150 word summary for your newsletter or website.
  • 04A discussion guide with 5 questions for small groups.
  • 05A caption for each post, ready to paste.

How it starts

We start by making a sample from your own audio.

  1. 01Step one

    We build a sample.

    We make it from your most recent public sermon audio, which we take from your podcast feed, before we ever write to you.

  2. 02Step two

    One clip is yours.

    One of the clips is finished and yours to post, free.

  3. 03Step three

    Then it's up to you.

    If the pack is useful, you subscribe and tell us where your sermon video lives.
