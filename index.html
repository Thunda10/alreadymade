Questions

Short answers.

Is this spam?

If we wrote to you, we built something specific for your company first. one message, one follow up, then we stop. Ask and we delete your preview and never write again.

Why did you pick us?

You publish good material and we thought we could make something useful from it. That is the whole reason.

How is it made so quickly?

Software does the production work. It reads your public site, pulls your real photos, facts and reviews, and lays out the pack from our templates. Automated checks then verify every claim against your own pages before you see anything.

Can I change things?

Yes. One round of edits by email is included, usually back within a day.

Who owns the files?

You do, once purchased. Your photos, words and reviews are yours either way.

What if I do not want it?

Do nothing. Your preview is deleted after 21 days, or sooner if you ask.

Do you take calls?

We work by email. It keeps prices low and puts every answer in writing.

Refunds?

You see the full work before you buy. If the delivered files do not match the preview, we fix it or refund you within 7 days.
