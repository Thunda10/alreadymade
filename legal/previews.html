Previews and removal

Last updated November 2026

Already Made is operated by JP Photography LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("we", "us"). Contact us through the request page or by email at .

How previews are made

We build each preview from public information only: the product photos, words, facts and reviews a company publishes on its own website, or for a Sunday Pack, the sermon audio a church already publishes in its podcast feed. We do not log in anywhere, buy anything or use private data.

Who can see a preview

Anyone with the private link. We send the link only to the company concerned, and preview pages are marked so search engines do not index them.

How long it stays up

Previews are deleted 21 days after we send them, along with the files behind them, unless you buy.

How to ask for removal

Reply to our message, or use the request page and write "remove" in the notes, or email . We delete the preview within 2 business days and do not write to you again.

Our own marketing

We do not reuse a company's preview in our own marketing without that company's permission. The Ad Pack and Booth Kit samples on this site are made for fictional brands. The Sunday Pack sample is made from a public domain recording of a sermon from 1858, read by a LibriVox volunteer and credited on the page.
