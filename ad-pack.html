Good to know

A few things we're careful about.

  • 01We only use images you already publish.
  • 02We don't retouch the product itself.
  • 03If your site has no reviews, we leave the review ad out. We won't write one.
  • 04Every pack includes one round of edits by email.
  • 05Could I make these with an ad tool? Yes. Ad tools start at about $20 to $40 a month. You set the tool up, sort the good ads from the bad and check the claims yourself. We charge $149 once. The ads arrive finished, and every line on them has been checked against your own site.
