About this page

This is a public sample page. A real preview page is unlisted: only people with the link can open it, and we send the link only to the company it was made for. A real page also shows the date we delete it and how to have it deleted sooner.

Questions

What do you need from me?

Only the link to your store. We take the products, names, prices and photos from your public site.

What if a price changes?

Reply with the change and we will send an updated catalog. One round of changes is included.

Can I print it?

Yes. Every page is US Letter with no bleed, so it prints on a home or office printer. Check every name and price against your store before you print.
