How to play the loop

The loop is a 1440x1080 MP4 file (H.264 video), 36 seconds long, at 30 frames per second, with no sound. To play it on repeat on a tablet, open the file in the tablet's video or photos app and turn on repeat or loop. It also plays on a booth TV the same way as below. To play it on repeat on a booth TV, copy the file to a USB stick, plug the stick into the TV, open the file from the TV's media player and turn on repeat (often called Repeat One or Loop). If the TV has no repeat setting, play it from a laptop set to repeat. The last frame leads straight back into the first, so the repeat has no visible jump.

Questions

What do you need from me?

Your store link, the market's name and dates, and your stall number if you have one. Product names, prices and photos come from your own site.

Does the pack use the market's logo?

No. The pack is for your own stall and promotion. It names the market and its dates exactly as you give them and does not use the market's logo or suggest that the market made it.

Can you change something?

Yes. Reply with any correction to a product, price, photo, stall or date and we will send updated files. One round of changes is included.
